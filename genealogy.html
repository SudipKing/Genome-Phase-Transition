How to interpret this

  • Members includes the founder and all recorded descendants.
  • Alive is the number alive at the moment the JSON was exported.
  • Extinct means no members of that founder lineage were alive at export. It does not mean the founder failed to reproduce.
  • Generation depth is based on parent links. A generation is one parent to child step.
  • This is ancestry evidence. To test phase transitions, later combine these measures with time series, genome composition, and population behavior.
