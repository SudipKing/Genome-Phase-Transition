Lineage distance vs genome distance

Pairs of organisms alive at the chosen tick. x = ticks back to their common ancestor; y = rules that differ between their genomes. Grey: all 252 conditions. Teal: only conditions cells actually meet at that tick. Lines are averages per bin. If genomes drift neutrally, differences keep growing with time apart; if selection holds the used rules in place, the teal line stays flat.

Extinctions and phase changes

Extinction events: every major clade (one that held ≥10% of living cells) lost at the same tick counts as one event, since nested clades on a branch die together. Click a row to jump there and inspect the largest clade lost.
