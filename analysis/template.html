    Each run over time

    Each panel shares one time axis. The top band shows the share of living cells in each colour. The middle line shows the average genome size, with the size of the consensus genome dashed. The bottom strip is the consensus genome: one column per sample, one row per condition (252 in total), coloured by the output most cells agree on. The top 84 rows are the fully surrounded conditions (no dead neighbours). Watch the strip at the moment the grid fills: the population's shared rules jump from conditions with empty neighbours (lower rows) to fully surrounded ones (top band).
