Organisms by distance to their nearest other genome

Mutants of a common parent sit 1–4 rules apart (a child differs from its parent in about 4 rules). Many organisms at 1–4 means the population is mostly mutant families.

Population distribution at t = 0

Representative (rep.) = the cluster's most common rule pattern. "Max dist. to rep." above t means the cluster was joined by chaining.

Lineage (from the .hxlg family tree): Founders = how many of the run's starting organisms the members descend from. Common ancestor = how long before the export the members' most recent common ancestor was born; Gens. = the most generations from any member back to it. A recent ancestor means the cluster is one mutant family; "none" means its members come from different founders.
